## Assessment

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Claim supported by evidence: **no** | **partially** |
| Confidence: **high** | **medium** |

The quantity of enriched uranium in the claim is supported by the IAEA. The claim that Iran had recently begun to weaponize is contradicted by the public independent assessments available at the time. The "a year … a few months" timeline cannot be tested from public sources. Confidence is medium because public assessments cannot rule out activity outside inspectors' view, and two quotes come from a single news report of an interview.

## After independent review

A second reviewer checked every item against its archived source and found the quotations and figures correct. The reviewer flagged that four sources were each reported by a single outlet, which Constitution 2.1 classes as Tier 3, and that four links in the sequence had no archived copy (2.5). Changes:

| Item | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Grossi interview quotes (former SUP-002 and CON-003) | Removed | Exclusive interview reported by one outlet; other outlets quoted that report. No independent source. |
