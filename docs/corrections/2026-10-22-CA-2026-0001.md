# Correction — CA-2026-0001

**Record:** CA-2026-0001, claim autopsy (English and Arabic)
**Date:** 22 October 2026
**Status before and after:** under review. The record was not published when corrected.

Every source cited by the record was reopened at its original. Several did not say what the record said they did. The record was rebuilt from the sources that were read. This note lists what changed and why.

## The claim

| Before | After | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "Iran posed an imminent nuclear threat requiring immediate military action." | Netanyahu's own words: Iran had taken "steps to weaponize this enriched uranium" and "could produce a nuclear weapon in a very short time. It could be a year. It could be within a few months -- less than a year." | The earlier text was a paraphrase that appears in none of the cited statements. The quote is from the Prime Minister's Office video of 13 June 2025. |
| Title: "Was Iran weeks away from a nuclear weapon?" | "Was Iran months from a nuclear weapon, and had it begun to weaponize?" | The speaker said "a year" or "a few months", not weeks. |
| 14 June 2025, "Address to the Knesset", strikes "launched eight days later" | 13 June 2025, recorded address announcing the operation as strikes began | The 14 June source is a press release issued after the strikes began. The IAEA (GOV/2025/50) dates the attacks to 13–24 June 2025. |

## Assessment

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Claim supported by evidence: **no** | **partially** |
| Confidence: **high** | **medium** |

The quantity of enriched uranium in the claim is supported by the IAEA. The claim that Iran had recently begun to weaponize is contradicted by the public independent assessments available at the time. The "a year … a few months" timeline cannot be tested from public sources. Confidence is medium because public assessments cannot rule out activity outside inspectors' view, and two quotes come from a single news report of an interview.
